How AT-RISK works

Feedback in. Clear support out.

AT-RISK connects a user's support feedback with an administrator's review and an explainable AI risk result. The user shares information; the administrator makes the prediction decision.

01

Create an account

Create an account with your name and password, then use the same details whenever you return.

02

Share feedback

Answer questions about your device, support experience, maintenance, training, cost and barriers.

03

Administrator reviews

Your feedback enters the admin review table. The administrator can inspect one entry or process all pending entries.

04

AI prediction runs

The trained model estimates abandonment risk only after the administrator clicks Predict.

05

Explanation is created

The system identifies relevant barriers and writes practical next steps instead of showing only a score.

06

User receives an update

The reviewed result appears in Your History with the risk status, explanation, action plan and report option.

What AT-RISK does not do

AT-RISK is a decision-support tool. It does not make a medical diagnosis, prescribe a device or replace professional judgement. The result is a prompt for a support conversation and follow-up.
